Saturday. Southampton. A record shop window. That was us.

First ever record shop gig. Playing in the window while the city walked past. We handed out free CDs, debuted a song with lyrics written the night before (*"Give It a Rest"* — yeah), and at one point Mark took the guitar out into the crowd and let people twist a knob — basically handing them a parameter on a live effect. Every person who touched it got a different sound out of it. The crowd were making the music. Sounded good. The people did well.

Three new songs in the set, one of which was written the night before. We always do this to ourselves. It's a recipe for disaster, some may call it professional suicide. This time we got away with it.

**Set:**
L.T.Y.M.W.R. / Replicas / The Bounty Hunter / T.T.O. / Blood on the Motorway / Within Isolation / Give It a Rest / Cold War / The Men with No Future

Video footage from the night is on its way — someone's promised us the files in the next few days, and we'll get it up here as soon as it lands.
